LIGHTING AND CAMERA SETTINGS (Misc role)

Unity 6, Universal 3D (URP) template. Map: 60 x 43 units.

Things to set up yourself: 

1. MAIN CAMERA (Far view)
   Position (0, 52, -52)   Rotation (45, 0, 0)   Field of View 30
   Clipping Planes Near 0.3, Far 500
   Rendering > Post Processing: TICKED (nothing in the Volume works without it)
   Environment > Background Type: Solid Color, #8FD0F5

2. AMBIENT AND FOG   (Window > Rendering > Lighting > Environment OR Ctrl 9)
   Environment Lighting Source: Gradient
     Sky      #9CC4E8 (Red: 156, Green: 196, Blue: 232)
     Equator  #C9D2B8 (Red: 201, Green: 210, Blue: 184)
     Ground   #6E7F55 (Red: 110, Green: 127, Blue: 85)

   Environment Reflections Intensity Multiplier: 0
   Fog (Other Settings): On, Mode Linear, Color #CFE3F0, Start 60, End 170

3. URP ASSET (Assets/Settings/PC_RPAsset, and check which asset Quality uses)
   HDR On, Anti-aliasing (MSAA) 4x
   Main Light: Per Pixel, Cast Shadows On, Main Light shadow resolution 4096 (use 2048 if the PC is slow)
   Shadows: Max Distance 90, Cascade Count 3, Soft Shadows On
